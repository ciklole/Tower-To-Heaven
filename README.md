# Tower-To-Heaven
Tower to Heaven is a parkour and obstacle course game where the goal is to make it all the way to the top of the tower.

The game has three levels. The first two are mainly parkour, while the last level is a large maze. There is lava throughout the tower, so falling can cost you your progress.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| W A S D | Move |
| Space | Jump |
| Left Shift | Run |
| Q | Dash |
| Right Click | Zoom |
| C | Crouch |
| V | Crouch lower |

## Stamina

There is a stamina system in the game.

Moving normally with WASD does not use stamina. Other movement actions do, so you need to keep an eye on your stamina when you're jumping, running, dashing, or using other movement abilities.

This is especially important during harder parts of the parkour sections, since running out of stamina at the wrong time can make an obstacle much harder to clear.

## The Levels

### Level 1 - Yellow

The first level is a parkour section with trees around the map. It is more open than the other levels, and you have to move through the obstacles while avoiding the lava.

The main challenge here is getting used to the movement and making it through the parkour without falling.

### Level 2 - Pink

The second level is another parkour section. It continues the climb upward and has a different layout from the first level.

The obstacles require more movement and careful timing, so managing your stamina becomes more important as you go.

### Level 3 - Red

The last level is a large maze.

Instead of just following a parkour course, you have to find your way through the maze and figure out where to go next. There are signs on the walls that give you directions, so pay attention to them.

The goal is to follow the signs and eventually find the stairs that lead to the end.

## Lava

The lava kills you when you touch it.

Try to be careful around jumps and obstacles, especially when you are low on stamina. Falling into the lava can send you back to the nearest checkpoint.

## Goal

Reach the top of the tower by completing all three levels.

You will need to:

- Complete the yellow parkour section.
- Complete the pink parkour section.
- Find your way through the red maze.
- Follow the signs in the maze.
- Find the stairs to the end.
- Reach the top.

## About the Game

Tower to Heaven is inspired by obby-style games, especially the kind of obstacle courses you can find on Roblox.

The idea is to keep climbing higher while the challenges change along the way. The first two levels focus on parkour, and the final level changes things up with a large maze.
